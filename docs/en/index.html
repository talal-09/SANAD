The problem

An AI result alone is not enough.

A radiologist needs to inspect the finding, adjust its measurement, document a decision, and know what happens next. SANAD brings those tasks into one clear experience.

BeforeScattered tools and files

Manual handoffs, unclear steps, and model output separated from the clinical decision.

→
With SANADOne clinical workflow

One required action at each stage, while the final decision remains with the clinician.

The workflow

Five steps, without confusing menus.

The system presents the action required now and moves the case to the next stage.

  1. 01Patient

    Select an existing record or register a new case.

  2. 02Imaging

    Upload a CT study and validate its files safely.

  3. 03Analysis

    Detect potential findings with a MONAI model.

  4. 04Clinical review

    Accept, correct, or reject each candidate.

  5. 05Follow-up

    Create a plan and notifications automatically.

Test results

High sensitivity, with clear room for improvement.

The model was tested on 84 held-out scans containing 102 nodules. It detected 97 and missed 5, while producing false positives that require radiologist review.

How should this result be read?

SANAD demonstrates that the technical workflow operates end to end. These numbers do not represent clinical validation or approval for clinical use.

Sensitivity95.1%97 of 102 nodules
Positive predictive value31.5%
False positives per scan2.51

The decision threshold was selected on validation data before it was applied to the test set.

Safety and responsibility

Designed around clinical review and case privacy.
